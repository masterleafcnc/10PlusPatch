# 101PlusPatch

The 1.00+ Patch is based on the R24u Patch by Masterleaf. See the [R21–R24 changelog](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LMfH_2jcBjOpHu4E0ZB7yAgqojlhO2nA55yLw-DXzMM/edit?tab=t.0) for the changes made in those versions.

## Changelog

### 1.00+ Patch — June 9, 2026

#### Global

- All changes from R24u and previous versions have been applied (see the changelog linked above).
- Multiplayer colors updated.
- New maps added: Desert Field [2]; Deadweight [2]; South Pacific [4]; Tiberium Freezes Over [4]; Dry Heat [4]; Arctic Circle [4]; Twisted Meadows [4]; Arabia [4]; Lost Temple [4]; The Second Tiberium [2]; Tiberium Desolation [6].
- New neutral building added on the map Sewer Wars [2].
- Maps updated: Sattelite Siege [2]; Island's Temple [2]; Tournament Island [2]; Sewer Wars [2]; Phosphor Winter [2]; Fall In Winter [2].

#### Nod

- The radius of the Catalyst missile reduced: 140 → 120.
- Purifier cost reduced: $2,800 / 28s → $2,700 / 27s.
- BlackHandSquad armor reduced: Cannon — 5%; Rocket — 50%; Grenade — 75%; Gun — 100%; Sniper — 100% → Cannon — 15%; Rocket — 55%; Grenade — 85%; Gun — 105%; Sniper — 100%.

#### Scrin

- Cost of all Stormriders reduced: $1,500 → $1,400.
- R17 Stormrider HP increased: 2,800 → 3,200.

#### GDI

- Watchtowers' stealth-detection radius increased: 100 → 200.

### Additional changes

#### Global

- Cost and build time of all Refineries reduced: $3,000 / 30s → $2,000 / 20s.
- Energy consumption of all Refineries reduced: 15⚡ → 6⚡.
- Cost and build time of all Harvesters reduced: $1,600 / 16s → $1,400 / 14s.
- Cost and build time of all Cranes increased: $1,300 / 13s → $1,500 / 15s.
- Cost and build time of all expand units increased: $1,300 / 13s → $1,500 / 15s.
- The movement speed of all Surveyors, Explorers, and Emissaries has been reduced: 66 → 50.
- The build radius of all Surveyors, Explorers, and Emissaries has been increased: 240 → 360.
- The deployment time of all Surveyors, Explorers, and Emissaries has been increased: 20s → 30s.
- The cost of building all Tiberium Silos has been increased: $100 → $500.
- After the sale of a Tiberium Silo, faction infantry appears.
- Pre-capture delay of all Engineers reduced: 3s → 1s.
- All War Factories now give a build radius of 250 (Refineries also give 250).
- Added the opportunity to sell a Mutant Hovel (+$500).
- Units now attack the Mutant Hovel automatically.

#### GDI

- All GDI receive a full Grenadier squad after a Command Post sale.

#### Nod

- The cost and construction time of a Shadow Team Squad increased: $800 / 8s → $900 / 9s.
- The movement speed of the Shadows reduced: 90 / 120 → 80 / 100 (on ground / flying).
- The cost of the "Red Shadows" ability reduced: $2,000 → $1,800.
- The 5-second delay has been removed when using the "Red Shadows" ability.
- The damage from the Shadows' "Explosive Charge" ability has been increased: 1,800 → 2,000.
- Nod and Marked of Kane receive a full Shadow Team squad after a Secret Shrine sale.
- Cooldown duration of the Redeemer's "Rage Generator" ability increased: 60s → 75s.
- The radius of the "Catalyst Missile" ability increased: 100 → 140 (will be reduced to 120 in the future).
- The bonus from the "Dozer Blades" upgrade for Scorpions reduced: 25% → 15%.
- The damage from the "Tiberium Vapor Bomb" ability increased: 9,000 → 10,000.

#### Scrin

- Shard Walker damage per projectile reduced: 120 / 160 → 100 / 140 (standard / Blue Shard upgrade).
- Shard Walker projectile speed increased: 500 → 600.
- After the sale or destruction of the Stasis Chamber, a Shock Trooper squad now appears (2/3 squads for sale and 1/3 squads for destruction).
